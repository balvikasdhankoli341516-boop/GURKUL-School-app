Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Gurukul Senior Secondary School, Dhankoli ("we," "our," or "the School") operates the Gurukul School Dhankoli mobile application and ERP portal. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information belonging to students, parents, applicants, and school staff.

1. Information We Collect

  • Student & Admission Information: Full name, class, date of birth, gender, category, blood group, parents' names, address, mobile number, email address, and government identification details required for official school records.
  • Uploaded Media (Photos & Signatures): Passport-size photographs and parent/student signatures uploaded voluntarily for admission forms, digital ID cards, admit cards, and report cards.
  • Academic & School Records: Attendance logs, exam marks, online CBT/Scholarship test responses, homework, and fee receipt records.
  • Location Data (School Bus GPS): Real-time location coordinates are used strictly when the authorized school bus driver or admin enables the Live Bus GPS Transmitter so parents and students can track the school bus route.

2. How We Use the Information

  • To process online school admissions and scholarship examination registrations.
  • To provide students and parents access to digital ID cards, admit cards, exam results, attendance records, live classes, homework, and fee receipts.
  • To send official school alerts, OTP verifications, and examination updates.
  • To provide real-time school bus location updates for student safety.

3. Data Storage and Security

All application data is securely transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database. We do not sell, rent, or share student or parent personal data with any unauthorized third-party marketing agencies.

4. Data Retention and Deletion / Correction

Student records are maintained for active academic sessions as required by educational regulations. Parents or students who wish to update, correct, or request the deletion of their application or account data may contact the School Administration directly using the contact details below.

5. Children's Privacy

This application is an official school management and learning portal meant to be used by students under the supervision of their parents/guardians and school authorities. Student login credentials are provided only after parent/school verification.
